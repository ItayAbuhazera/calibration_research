# Conformal Risk Control

## Why this matters

Provides a formal language for converting predictive scores into decisions with
risk targets rather than optimizing calibration metrics alone.

## Link

Use this when the research question becomes:
**At the same controlled risk, does a richer internal signal permit more useful
actions / higher coverage?**

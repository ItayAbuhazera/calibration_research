# Serial method tests without discriminating outcomes

## Failure

A sequence of method variants can consume development exposure while leaving
the central explanation unresolved. A result that only says “this variant did
not clear a threshold” is useful, but does not justify an automatic next
variant, a mechanism claim, or a broad information-ceiling claim.

## Where observed

The representation-correction line contains scoped stops (the layer pilot,
residual study, atlas and fixed-gate study) alongside a continuing broader
question. Their historical records are preserved in [[Current Evidence -
Representation-Based Correction Program]]; this note changes neither their
verdicts nor their authorization.

## Competing explanations

- The tested readout/capacity, fitting budget, or selection rule is inadequate
  although useful information could remain.
- The added representation has no practical incremental utility beyond a
  matched output-only system in the tested regime.
- Apparent gains or nulls reflect reuse of development cells, regularization,
  or an inadequately matched control rather than the proposed mechanism.

## Why the workflow can fail

- Reusing development cells is valid exploration, but repeated performance is
  not untouched confirmation and adaptive choices must be disclosed.
- Calling a target-trained probe an “oracle” or “information ceiling” hides
  its feature family, fitting budget, and evaluation scope.
- Positive correctness/proper-score prediction can be mistaken for net
  decision utility; replacement correctness and W/H/U accounting are separate.
- A crossed-zero interval is not no effect, while an interval below a
  prespecified useful scale may justify a practical stop. Neither rule alone
  establishes mechanism.
- “Another layer, architecture, gate, or dataset” is not a follow-up rationale
  without a new prediction and discriminating contrast.

## What would falsify this failure mode

A bounded card that names competing explanations, has predictions that differ,
uses an appropriate matched control, declares access/exposure, and maps all
outcomes to an allocation decision can justify a next experiment. It need not
ban iterative development or require a theorem/oracle first.

## Guardrail

Use [[Representation Correction Exposure Ledger]] and the canonical
[`research workflow`](../../GeometricFullCalibration/docs/research_workflow.md)
before allocating new work. Stop a method branch when warranted, retain the
broader question as open only when a specific future contrast could change a
decision, and record “unknown” where explanations remain indistinguishable.

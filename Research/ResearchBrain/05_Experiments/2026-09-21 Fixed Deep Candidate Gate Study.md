# 2026-09-21 — Fixed deep-layer candidate, gate information and effective sample size

Follows [[2026-09-21 Representation Atlas Program]] (verdicts preserved; corrections appended there). Hypothesis: [[H-GATE-01 Candidate selection versus gate utility mismatch]].

## Design (facts)
Fixed candidate: `layer3.22` → 2×2 avg-pool → flatten (4096-d, no projection) → L2 → exact kNN k=50 over the 45 000 bank, p_geo smoothed. Provenance: layer-pilot NLL-greedy L=1 pick (GAP additive-β readout, different objective); NOT clean-selected under the atlas rule (clean-selection net utility ≈ −0.16). Choice is informed by atlas outcomes already seen. Controls: atlas-selected layer4 GAP and output-space (Mahalanobis) candidates, full budget. Gates C0/C1/Z0/Z1 (ridge on disagreements, λ∈{0.01,1,100}, θ grid + never), prefixes n∈{625,1250,2500} × 3 stratified orders. Recomputed neighbours reproduce atlas Stage-A on 31 000 queries (same j 100 %; identical sets 99.997 %).

## Audit results (facts, both checkpoints)
* Effective events at n=2500 (W/H/U of fit disagreements): deep 873 (123/470/280) seed 2, 908 (126/487/295) seed 4; layer4 GAP 144 / 69; output 83 / 74. **The 52–151-event limitation was specific to the layer4/output candidates**; the deep candidate has 6–10× more disagreements (even n=625 gives ≈210–240) but H:W ≈ 3.8:1, so W ≈ 125.
* Fixed-candidate standalone: net utility −14.2/−15.4 pp clean, −12.4/−13.6 pp macro-12; repair W/base-errors 20–21 % clean, 12.9/13.4 % corruption; harm H/base-correct 24.7/26.6 % clean, 40.2/42.0 % corruption; flips per example 54–56 %. The atlas "10–14 % repair / 38–52 % harm" were corruption macro figures, same seeds/cells/rows as the 2.5 % logit-control repair (harm 1.9 %), but the deep sites shown were the target-label-peak sites (layer3.19–3.22) among 34; repair alone is not net utility (per-flip W/(W+H+U) 9.8 % deep vs 21 % output).
* Oracle union (+0.8 pp): exact set = base, out_alone, VS, MS, gap@layer4.1|4.2, grid2@layer4.1, spp@layer4.2; deep-layer3 spatial NOT included (reproduced +0.82/+0.86). With the fixed deep candidate the oracle increment is +4.7 pp, but this is an evaluation-only label oracle over a classifier that is 12 pp worse than base and says nothing about feasible gating.
* Numerics: the benchmark base path is `PyTorchModelAdapter` with a `torch.device` (autocast NOT active), library-default TF32 convs, batch 128. Re-running that exact path reproduces the benchmark argmax with **0 mismatches** (13 seed-2 and 10 seed-4 conditions, repeat identical); atlas strict FP32 differs by 101 / 80 images (all near-ties); batch 256 gives 16; fp16 autocast is worse (180 / 135). So the TF32 explanation is verified, not inferred. Effect on this study: all methods use one FP32 logit artifact; ≤2 (seed 2) / 11 (seed 4) mismatch images across all cells sit on deep-Z1 interventions; worst-case bound on any paired gain vs base ≈ 0.08 pp macro (comparable to the seed-2 Z1 gain +0.075 pp). Seed-4 remaining cells: deferred jobs 21537819/20/21 (afterany array 21533078).
* Latency (same frozen config, interleaved, synchronized, resident banks, median): batch 1 backbone 9.95 ms, hooked 9.82 (hook overhead within noise; not negative), search-only 0.93, full pipeline 12.6 ms (+26 %); batch 256 57.6 → 68.4 ms (+19 %). Storage 0.055 GB/checkpoint neighbour files for the fixed candidate vs resident bank 0.74 GB (4096-d) — the earlier 1.7 GB (stored neighbour/logit files of 102 configurations) and 7.7 GB (resident layer4.2 SPP bank) are different quantities.

## Gate results (facts; 2 checkpoints; descriptive paired bootstrap over 10 000 image IDs)
* Held-out utility regression has real but compact signal: held-out MSE / constant-MSE ≈ 0.78–0.83 (seed 2) and 0.73–0.79 (seed 4) for **all** families including 5-feature C0; no improvement with n or m.
* Primary Z1 − Z0 (deep, n=2500): clean +0.10 pp [−0.04,+0.23] / +0.13 [−0.17,+0.42]; corruption macro −0.04 [−0.09,+0.01] / −0.04 [−0.13,+0.05]. Not consistent.
* Deep Z1 vs base: clean +0.08 [0.00,0.16] / +0.50 [0.29,0.71]; macro +0.075 / +0.21 pp (coverage 0.3 % / 3.0 %). vs never(native DAC): same, with raw NLL worse (+0.001 / +0.034). vs output-candidate gate (out Z1): clean +0.11 [−0.07,0.29] / +0.35 [0.06,0.64]; macro +0.03 / −0.02. vs layer4 Z1: macro +0.02 / −0.07. C0 (output evidence only) on the deep candidate gives +0.18 / +0.19 macro ≈ Z1: geometric features add nothing demonstrated. Practical targets (+0.5 pp over base, +0.25 over output control) not met on the 12-cell macro; seed-4 clean +0.50 is the only outlier and does not transfer.
* Utility bins (edges from clean-selection): predicted utility ranks realized utility monotonically on selection rows, clean test and corruptions, but the top bin's mean utility ≈ 0 (clean 0.00 / +0.04; noise −0.05/−0.04; JPEG +0.11/+0.07): no large positive-utility region.
* Learning curve: no growing benefit with n or m; generalization gap shrinks (train ≈ held-out ratio at 2500) but net gain does not rise; orders are sensitivity, not replication.
* Probability quality: gated pipelines never beat native DAC's raw NLL/Brier (seed 2 macro NLL 2.316 vs Z1 2.317; seed 4 2.207 vs 2.241); clean-fitted temperature again worsens corruption NLL for DAC/gates.

## Interpretation (labelled)
Supported (development): candidate alternatives with rankable utility exist; an intervention pocket of ≈0.1–0.5 pp exists on clean data. Not supported: that geometric gate features add value over output evidence; that gains transfer across shift; a sample-complexity trend. Consistent with outcome cases 2/3/4 of the brief; not case 1 or 6. Unresolved: whether the limit is candidate quality (H:W 3.8:1), gate family, or shift; small effects vs unresolved prediction differences.
**Not established:** absence of information; that more clean labels would not help (flat curve ≠ proof); anything beyond two checkpoints and historically exposed roles.

## Recommendation (see report)
Stop this fixed candidate/gate formulation; any larger-supervision study would need a different justification than event counts (they were not the limit for the deep candidate).

### Learning curves and utility bins — what they do and do not support (existing artifacts, not re-derived)
`results/fixed_gate/report/{learning_curve,utility_bins}.json` and `report/figures/{learning_curve_vs_n,learning_curve_vs_m,utility_bins}.png`.
* **Useful alternative labels — supported (weak):** held-out MSE / constant-predictor MSE ≈ 0.73–0.83 at every n and m, both checkpoints, all four families including 5-feature C0. The candidate's disagreements carry real, if compact, signal about D.
* **Predictable intervention utility — supported (ranking only):** score-quintile bins (edges from clean-selection rows) rank realized mean utility monotonically on selection rows, clean test, and every corruption family in both checkpoints.
* **A held-out positive-utility intervention region — NOT supported:** the top score bin's mean utility is ≈0 on clean test (0.00 / +0.04) and on corruption families (range −0.05 to +0.11); no bin reaches a reliably large positive value. Ranking working is a separate, weaker claim than a usable positive-utility region existing, and this data does not support the stronger claim.
* **Incremental value from geometry — NOT supported:** C1 vs C0 and Z1 vs Z0 are within noise on held-out clean rows and on the 12-cell macro in both checkpoints; the primary Z1−Z0 contrast interval includes 0 in both checkpoints and both scopes (clean, macro-12).
* **Transfer to corruption — NOT supported / inconsistent:** seed 4's clean gain (deep Z1 vs base +0.50 pp) drops to +0.21 pp macro-12; seed 2 is small and consistent in sign (+0.08 / +0.075 pp) but both checkpoints' Z1−Z0 macro-12 interval includes 0. No learning-curve growth with n or m in either seed.
None of these five points is inferred from another; each is read off its own artifact.

## Final recommendation
**Close this fixed-candidate/gate formulation.** The event-count limitation is ruled out (deep candidate: 873–908 fit disagreements); numerical precision differences are ruled out as an explanation (bound ≤0.014 pp vs observed effects of similar or smaller magnitude); geometric features add no demonstrated value over output evidence; no held-out region has usable positive utility; gains do not consistently transfer to corruption. This is a negative result on its own terms, not merely an underpowered one — the reasons a larger-supervision study is not justified by this outcome alone. Concrete evidence that would reopen the question: a demonstration (not performed here) that the H:W ≈ 3.8:1 imbalance is a property of a specific fixable mechanism (e.g., a different labelled comparison set showing separable helpful/harmful subpopulations within the deep candidate's disagreements) rather than of the candidate/gate family tested.

The identity ΔAccuracy = P(A)·E[D∣A] holds exactly (F/N × (W−H)/F = (W−H)/N by construction, not as an empirical approximation). **On the actual, narrow, frozen intervention set, empirical utility is clearly positive** — strongly so in seed 2 (+22.6% per intervention, decisive precision 70%) and positive but weaker in seed 4 (+7.2% per intervention, decisive precision 58.5%). This set (0.33%/2.98% of rows) is far narrower than the top score-quintile bin reported earlier (20% of rows, mean utility ≈0): the near-zero top-bin reading and the positive intervention-set reading describe different, non-nested sets and are not in tension. This does not establish a population guarantee or license selecting a new threshold from these counts.

**C. Sample-size claims — corrected.** The deep candidate's 873–908 fit disagreements (vs. 52–151 for the layer4/output candidates) show the earlier low-event explanation does not apply to this candidate; it does **not** show sample size is ruled out as a limiting factor for any effect of interest, and the flat learning curve (no growth from n=625 to 2500) is consistent with several explanations, none isolated here: an effect too small for this n to resolve, a genuine ceiling, or a mismatched gate family. There is **no current empirical justification for prioritizing a larger-supervision continuation** — that is a scope limitation, not evidence either for or against more data helping. Per-contrast disposition: Z1−Z0 and deep-Z1-vs-output-gate are genuinely uncertain (intervals include 0, not evidence against a real effect); deep-Z1-vs-base fails the +0.5 pp practical threshold with intervals that also exclude the threshold value itself (statistical evidence against *that magnitude*, not against a smaller real effect, which the interval is consistent with).

**D. MSE and alternatives — scope corrected.** Held-out MSE ≈0.73–0.83 of the constant-predictor baseline (`results/fixed_gate/report/learning_curve.json`) supports that D is *predictable* relative to a constant predictor; it does not by itself establish useful alternative labels or positive intervention utility, since a model that improves MSE by confidently predicting harmful actions (D=−1) would also lower this ratio. The existence of repairs is established separately, by the raw W counts in the tables above (never by MSE). Oracle-union evidence, score-bin ranking evidence, thresholded actual-intervention evidence (item B), and proper-score (NLL/Brier) evidence are four distinct kinds of evidence and are kept separate in this note; none is inferred from another.

**E. Numerical bounds — corrected magnitude and documented scope.** The quoted bounds (0.0017 pp seed 2, 0.0142 pp seed 4) are **≈44× and ≈15× smaller** than the corresponding deep-Z1-vs-base gains (+0.075 / +0.213 pp) — **not** "two to three orders of magnitude" as stated in the 2026-09-22 closure section above; that phrase is corrected here. Exact scope of the bound (from `atlas/fg_numerics_effect.py`): for each corruption cell, it counts test images where an atlas-FP32 (batch-250) base prediction disagrees with an independently-run benchmark-path (TF32 conv, batch-128) base prediction, restricted to the subset of those images that also sit in the **already-frozen** deep-Z1 gate's actual intervention set (the gate, candidate neighbours, and p_geo were computed once, entirely under the atlas FP32 pipeline, and were never re-extracted under the benchmark's numerical configuration); it then sums the worst-case (fully adversarial) accuracy-pp effect over the 12 cells. This is a **within-run, conditional-on-the-frozen-artifacts sensitivity bound**, not a cross-pipeline reproducibility guarantee: it says nothing about whether refitting the gate or re-extracting the layer3.22 features under TF32/batch-128 precision would select a different λ/θ or a different disagreement set, and it was computed for accuracy only (pp), not for NLL or Brier (ordinary score units, not pp — no NLL/Brier bound of this kind exists in the artifacts). Within-study paired comparisons (the contrast table above) use one consistent FP32 batch-250 logit artifact throughout, by construction (`docs/fixed_gate_study_spec.md` §3); the benchmark array is never mixed into them, only used for this separate, narrower-scope sensitivity check.

**F. Transfer — restated.** Both checkpoints keep a small, interval-excludes-zero positive gain over base under corruption (item A); a smaller corruption gain than the clean gain (seed 4: +0.50 pp clean → +0.21 pp macro-12) is not by itself evidence of failed transfer. The sharper, better-supported finding is: no consistent incremental advantage over the matched output-candidate/layer4 controls (both intervals include 0), and no demonstrated geometric-gate-feature benefit (Z1−Z0 interval includes 0), in either checkpoint. Two historically-exposed checkpoints and four fixed corruption families do not establish broad robustness of either the small positive gain or its absence.

**G. Controls — restated for precision.** Every candidate arm here (deep, layer4-GAP, output-space) is itself hidden- or output-derived and acts as the pipeline's chosen action; Z0/C0 on the deep or layer4 hidden candidate still use hidden information to pick that action; they are not logit-only pipelines. Z1−Z0 isolates the marginal value of the four added geometric features *given* a hidden candidate is already the acting alternative. The separate output-candidate pipeline (`out|*`) is the whole-system control for "would output-space information alone have done as well." Both distinctions are preserved throughout this note; a reader must not conflate "output-evidence gate on a hidden candidate" with "output-only pipeline."

**H. Frozen conclusion — scope restated.** This fixed layer3.22/2×2 candidate and its four ridge-gate families, at n≤2500 clean-fit rows, are closed under the criteria in `docs/fixed_gate_study_spec.md`. This does **not** extend to: all representation information in this or other layers, nonlinear or learned (non-ridge) readouts, other prototype/kNN configurations, or clean-only shift correction in general. No untested alternative (larger supervision, a different gate family, a different candidate) is presented anywhere in this vault as the demonstrated explanation for the negative result; where one is mentioned (item C) it is explicitly labelled as an open, unruled-in-or-out possibility.

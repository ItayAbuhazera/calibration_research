## Cross-project pattern worth watching

A recurring motif is emerging:

**having a useful uncertainty / geometric signal is easier than knowing when and
how it should change the system's action.**

Evidence so far:
1. full-vector methods can gain accuracy by changing decisions, while top-label
   anchors can suppress those gains;
2. under corruption, geometry has oracle complementarity, but a validation-fitted
   reliability gate does not know when to use it;
3. scalar calibration methods can improve probabilities while being structurally
   incapable of changing the decision at all.

## Stress test of that motif - currently WEAKLY supported

Of the three supporting points above:

- **#3 is a theorem** (a positive scalar temperature preserves logit order). It
  holds regardless of any experiment, so it is a control, not evidence.
- **#1 is partly by construction**: anchored methods are designed not to move the
  argmax. The empirical residue is only that the forbidden changes were net
  beneficial on one exploratory CIFAR-100 batch.
- **#2 is the only real empirical test**, and it has two open failure paths:
  the 4.4 pp oracle headroom may be what any two disagreeing predictors produce
  (no null baseline exists), and the gate may have failed because `k_vote=200`
  rather than the pre-registered 50.

Treat the motif as a hypothesis to be tested by the two experiments in the
research queue below, not as an established pattern. This is a research motif,
not yet a novelty claim.

## Strongest unresolved questions

1. Do the existing reliability features predict "geometry is better than head"

4. Under what controlled conditions does geometry begin to justify decision

## Archaeology addendum (2026-09-15)

A read-only archaeology pass over the two historical repositories
(`GeometricCalibration`, `GeometricInternalCalibration`) recovered three
historical precursors of this week's "reliability of the signal itself"
question:

**A.** The original [[Geometric Separation]] project already showed a
clean-to-corruption reversal for some geometric confidence mappings: a
clean-fitted raw-pixel geometric calibrator became *worse* than uncalibrated
across all matched CIFAR-10/CIFAR-100 rows under the repository's synthetic
(not CIFAR-C) corruption protocol. See
[[Clean-fitted geometric confidence mappings can reverse under synthetic corruption]].

**B.** The RGC repository contains a failed geometry-based layer selector: an
unsupervised composite geometric score, evaluated on 12 recovered
AugMix/CIFAR-10 experiments, was the *worst* of four compared layer-choice
strategies — worse than even a naive fixed-default layer. "Good-looking"
geometry did not reliably imply good calibration. (Evidence for this one is
recoverable only from a non-canonical backup directory; the canonical repo
never wired the module in.) See
[[Composite geometry scores do not reliably select calibration layers]].

**C.** The RGC repository's feature/algorithm swap experiments show that
representation information and the rule that consumes it cannot be treated
independently: DAC's density-aware mechanism collapses sharply (CIFAR-100
only) when fed randomized-coordinate features instead of its native layer
features, even though RGCC pairs the same style of randomized coordinates
with its own scoring/mapping successfully. See
[[DAC can collapse on randomized coordinate features]].

**These observations make the current "reliability of the signal itself"
question historically recurrent, but they do NOT yet establish a single
common mechanism.** The representation, the corruption/shift protocol, the
task (routing vs. layer choice vs. feature substitution), and the metric
differ across all three, and no cross-project null or unifying experiment has
been run. See [[Research Lineage]] for the full lineage account and the
repeated-questions list.

## G3 Headroom Null addendum (2026-09-15)

Research queue item 1 above is done: see [[2026-09-15 G3 Headroom Null]].
Geometry's oracle-union headroom clears a disagreement-matched
no-information null by roughly 10x (ruling out "any disagreeing predictor
would do this"), which sharpens the motif above — but a second,
independently-trained head clears the same null by roughly 20x, about
double geometry's own margin. The current safest statement of the
cross-generation motif is therefore weaker than "having the signal is
easier than knowing when to act": G1/G2 were argmax-preserving and never
tested decision value, so that phrasing is not yet supported. Prefer:
"across multiple generations, we repeatedly observe a gap between an
optimistic oracle showing apparent exploitable structure and the practical
rule used to select or act on that structure" — and even this still needs
the geometry-vs-independent-head gap checked with paired CIs before being
treated as settled.

Research queue item 2 (`k_vote=50` re-run): ran a fresh, explicitly
non-reproducing univariate audit of the recomputable vote-based features
instead — [[2026-09-15 G3 k_vote Audit]]. Result: small and mixed (margin/
vote-concentration improve ~1-2 AUROC points at k=50; agreement gets very
slightly worse), and absolute discrimination stays weak-to-moderate
(0.49-0.66 AUROC) at either k. **k_vote does not look like the main lever
behind the gate's failure.** (This session initially reported the gate's
fitting code as missing from the repo entirely — that was wrong; it is
`GeometricFullCalibration/rgc-shift.zip`, a gitignored separate package,
found and corrected later the same day. See
[[2026-09-15 G3 Controlled Complementarity]].)

## Controlled complementarity addendum (2026-09-15, later same day)

Chased the independent-head mechanism from the headroom-null result:
geometry's standalone accuracy (~0.29-0.30) is far below both the head's and
an independent second head's (~0.487 each), flagged as a correlational, not
yet identified, explanation. Tried to control for it directly in
[[2026-09-15 G3 Controlled Complementarity]] — **landed inconclusive**, not
resolved either way: no existing predictor family in this repo spans
geometry's accuracy range (everything else available clusters at
0.48-0.50, a full 20-point gap above where geometry sits), so a
strength-matched comparison cannot currently be built from existing
artifacts. One in-range data point (seed 1's geometry, which happens to sit
at ~0.48 accuracy) is fit almost exactly by the strength-only model and is
stable under jackknife resampling — mildly suggestive that strength alone
explains the gap — but it is a single, already-anomalous seed, not a
generalizable test.

Side finding: seed 1's anomaly (see
[[Seed 1 occupies a qualitatively different kNN regime]]) is not explained
by checkpoint strength (seed 1's checkpoint is the *weakest* of the 5) — it
correlates with a 5-8x higher neighbourhood concentration/purity than seeds
2-5, computed directly from cache using the now-confirmed-correct
`rgc_shift` formulas. Also found that `perclass` and `globalknn` exports
share byte-identical neighbour-retrieval arrays and differ only in the vote
rule — the two "geometry" arms used throughout this research line are more
correlated by construction than their names suggest.

The minimal next experiment (extracting raw test-cell embeddings to fit one
real, deliberately-weakened same-representation readout in the 0.30-0.45
accuracy band) is specified in that card but not run.

## Corrected k_vote audit and synthetic matched-strength null addendum (2026-09-15, later same day)

Two of the cheapest remaining uncertainties in this line were resolved
without any new GPU inference, retraining, or new routing method.

**k_vote, corrected.** The `rgc_shift` package's exact source (pinned
`ff81032f...`, unzipped from `rgc-shift.zip`) was read directly rather than
inferred: `head_neighbour_agreement` and `neighbourhood_concentration` are
real `rgc_shift` functions; `neighbour_margin` and `knn_radius` turn out to
be *this repo's own* `export_recoverability.py` code, not `rgc_shift` at
all — a more precise correction than the same-day correction already
recorded in [[2026-09-15 G3 Controlled Complementarity]]. The original
[[2026-09-15 G3 k_vote Audit]] is now marked SUPERSEDED (its `agreement`
feature was a self-invented binary proxy for a continuous quantity) and
replaced by [[2026-09-15 G3 k_vote Audit Corrected]], which adds full
descriptive statistics, saturation rates, and Spearman(k50,k200) that the
original never had. Verdict is still **k_vote CHANGE MINOR** overall, but
with the real formula, `head_neighbour_agreement` now shows a clearly
*negative* delta at k=50 (2-3.65 AUROC points worse for seeds 2-5) rather
than the earlier near-zero result — a sign the earlier binary approximation
had actually washed out. Saturation is now precisely quantified: ~53% of
seed 1's samples sit at the absolute maximum of all three recomputable
features at k=50, vs. ~0% for seeds 2-5.

**Synthetic matched-strength null.** [[2026-09-15 G3 Controlled
Complementarity]]'s inconclusive verdict stemmed from no real predictor
family spanning geometry's accuracy regime. Rather than running the
same-representation readout that card specified (which needs new compute),
this experiment deliberately weakened cached independent-head *logits*
(temperature-scaled sampling, additive Gaussian noise — both fixed-RNG,
Monte-Carlo-replicated, and disclosed-compute-driven grid adjustments made
before any results were inspected) to build a generic matched-strength,
matched-disagreement null. Result: [[2026-09-15 G3 Synthetic
Matched-Strength Null]] finds geometry **consistent with the matched
synthetic null** for the seeds-2-5 aggregate — excess headroom/rescue
precision within ~0.35 percentage points of zero, an order of magnitude
below the 3.80-4.89pp raw headroom that motivated this whole line. This is
a partial resolution of Controlled Complementarity's inconclusive verdict
(the *generic*-degradation version of strength-matching now resolves
toward B; the *same-representation* version remains untested and is the
one open path to a stronger claim). Seed 1 again stands apart: its
disagreement rate is below the entire synthetic grid's achievable range,
so matching failed outright for it — independent, converging evidence
(alongside the concentration/purity numbers) that its anomaly is not
reproducible by generic head-degradation.

# Weekly Research Synthesis — 2026-W39

(Created 2026-09-23. Earlier entries dated 2026-09-21/22 live in [[2026-W38]]; nothing there was rewritten.)

## New observations

* [[Target-fitted stacking of logits and layer3.22 probe logits recovers a material increment that clean-fitted stacking on matched budgets does not]] — Stage 0, a target-supervised diagnostic on exposed development cells (checkpoints 2 and 4): target-fitted `q_ZP` beats target-fitted `q_Z` by +4.23 / +3.98 pp macro accuracy; matched clean-fitted stacking does not (gap +3.35 / +2.91 pp at 8k×1). Card: [[2026-09-22 Stage 0 Probe-Logit Increment Study]].

## Prior evidence now connected

* The closed fixed-gate, residual, layer-pilot and atlas studies each found small or null clean-fitted effects; Stage 0 adds that a *target-supervised* readout of the same layer's probe logits does find a material increment, with a large part (≈42–44 %, post-hoc) attributable to plain target recalibration of the logits. The two are compatible: clean supervision at matched budgets does not recover it. This does not reopen any closed study.

## Working guesses refuted this week (interpretation, post-hoc)

* "The intermediate probe is simply more robust" — refuted by 0b (probe alone below base in all conditions).
* "The gap is one scalar trust weight" — a scalar blend recovers 26–29 % (target labels) / 8–11 % (clean labels) of Δ_T.

## Strongest unresolved questions

Why the useful combination is not recovered from clean supervision (non-identifiability, clean-fit regularization, shift of the probe-logit distribution, readout mismatch); whether the gain is specific to `layer3.22` or generic to fusing any comparably informative predictor. Proposed, not established: [[H-STAGE0-01 Layer3.22 probe evidence is redundant with the logits on clean data but complementary under corruption, and clean supervision cannot identify the useful combination]].
